### `pr`

The "shape of the change" content is [Dex Horthy](https://github.com/dexhorthy)'s
[`show-me`](https://github.com/humanlayer/skills/blob/main/plugins/show-me/skills/show-me/SKILL.md)

of a live conversation. Recorded in the skill's own
[`pr/CREDITS.md`](skills/pr/CREDITS.md) and in its frontmatter `metadata.credits`.

### Skills referencing `/setup-matt-pocock-skills`

`to-spec`, `wayfinder`, and `code-review` instruct the reader to run
`/setup-matt-pocock-skills`, and a larger part of this collection (the
grilling / spec / writing / architecture families) matches the structure of that
distributed skill set. The upstream repository and its license have **not** been
confirmed from these files alone. Provenance to verify before any public use.
